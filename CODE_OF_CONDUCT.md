# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever
their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting other viewpoints and experience;
- giving and accepting criticism of the work gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone's private information without their permission;
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities

The maintainers are responsible for making these standards clear and for acting fairly
when they are not met. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban, temporarily or
permanently, a contributor whose behaviour they find inappropriate.

## Scope

This code applies in the project's spaces and wherever someone represents the project
in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact address on the
[GitHub profile of the project owner](https://github.com/telmomm). Reports are reviewed
and kept confidential. A maintainer who does not follow or enforce this code in good
faith may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.
